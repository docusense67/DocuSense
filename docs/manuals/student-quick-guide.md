# Student quick guide

Request your Transcript of Records (TOR) or Certificate of Enrollment (COE) online, then download it.

## Sign in

1. Enter your **Email** and **Password**, then click **Sign in**.
2. The first time, you will see **Set a new password**. Enter the temporary password from the registrar in **Current password**. Enter a new password of at least 10 characters. Click **Save password**.

## Check your records first

**My records** lists your grades and GWA per term. "(grades not final)" means the term is not yet locked. Report errors to the registrar **before** requesting.

## Request a document

1. Under **Request a document**, choose the **Document**.
2. Type the **Purpose** (for example, Employment) and the number of **Copies** (1 to 10).
3. Click **Submit**. You get a reference number, such as REQ-2026-000012, and a note on what happens next: the registrar approves your request first, then your document is prepared and checked.

If a document shows "(not available)", the reason appears below the form, for example "Incomplete grade in IT101".

## Track your request

Your requests appear under **My requests**.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | Received and waiting for the registrar's approval (and payment, if your school charges a fee). |
| Approved | The registrar approved it. Your document is being prepared and checked. If your school uses pickup, wait for the registrar to release it. |
| Completed | Ready. Click **Download PDF**. |
| Rejected | Not accepted. The reason appears under the status. |
| Cancelled | You or the registrar cancelled it. |
| Expired | Your download period ended. The document is still valid. |
| Revoked | The registrar withdrew the document. It will no longer pass the school's check. |

You can **Cancel** a request while it is Pending, or Approved until the registrar has checked the document.

## Download and print

1. When the status is **Completed**, click **Download PDF**. The PDF opens in a new tab.
2. Print at 100% scale. Do not resize, edit or crop it; the QR code and footers are checked later.

You can download until the date shown ("Download until ..."). After a download, the request shows "Downloaded N time(s), last ...".

## Common questions

- **The download link stopped working.** Each link lasts 5 minutes. Click **Download PDF** again.
- **Status is Expired.** The 30-day download period ended. Ask the registrar to reopen it. Your document is still valid.
- **My request was Rejected because of an INC grade.** A TOR needs every completed term to have a final grade. Settle the incomplete grade, then submit a new request.
- **"A TOR request is already pending."** Only one open request per document is allowed.
- **I was signed out.** After 30 minutes without activity you are signed out. Sign in again.
- **"Invalid email or password…"** Check your email and password. After 5 wrong tries, your account locks for 15 minutes, then unlocks by itself. The registrar can unlock it sooner.
